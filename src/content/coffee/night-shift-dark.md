Some mornings ask for nuance. Others ask for a coffee that shows up and does the
job. Night Shift is the second kind. We roast a dense Guatemalan and an earthy
wet-hulled Sumatra well into second crack for that classic dark-roast pull —
molasses, dark chocolate, a little smoke. It's the cup that got a lot of our
regulars in the door, and we're not about to retire it.
