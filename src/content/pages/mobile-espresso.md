## The bar comes to you

The Water Bearer mobile cart is a complete espresso setup on wheels: a commercial
machine, a grinder dialed to our house blend, and a barista who actually knows
what they're doing behind it. We pull the same shots, steam the same milk, and
pour the same house chai you'd get at the shop — just wherever you need us.

## Where we go

- **Weddings & receptions** — a caffeine lifeline for your guests, and a much
  better photo than a gas-station cup.
- **Corporate & office mornings** — impress a client, launch a product, or just
  make a Monday bearable.
- **Farmers markets & pop-ups** — catch us around town all season.
- **Private parties & showers** — birthdays, baby showers, graduations.
- **Film & production sets** — early calls, long days, real coffee.

## How it works

Tell us the date, the headcount, and where you're parked us, and we'll put
together a quote. We bring everything — power needs are minimal, and we can run
off a standard outlet or our own setup for off-grid spots. Most events run two
to three hours of continuous service; we'll help you figure out the right window
for your guest count.
