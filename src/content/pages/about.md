## The whole idea, in one sentence

The coffee in your cup was roasted a few feet from where it was poured, by the
same person who poured it.

That's not a marketing line for us — it's the entire way the shop is built. Most
cafés buy their beans from a roaster somewhere else and their syrups from a
distributor. We don't. **We roast our coffee in house, and everything else —
the chai, the syrups, the cold brew, the sauces — we make from scratch.** When
you order a vanilla latte, someone here cooked the vanilla syrup. When you order
a pour-over, someone here decided how dark to take that lot last week.

## One person, both jobs

Coffee usually splits into two worlds that never meet: the roaster in a warehouse
who never sees a customer, and the barista at the bar who never sees green
coffee. We put them in the same apron.

Our roaster spends the early mornings at the drum roaster dialing in the week's
batches, then moves to the espresso machine and spends the day pulling shots of
the exact coffee they just roasted. That loop — roast it, pull it, taste it
across the counter, adjust next week — is short and honest. It's why the house
blend keeps getting better, and it's why we can tell you precisely what's in your
cup, because we were standing there when it was made.

## Made from scratch, actually

"House-made" gets thrown around a lot, so here's what it means here: our chai is
whole spices simmered in small batches, not a pump of syrup from a jug. Our
vanilla syrup is real vanilla beans and sugar. Our cold brew steeps for sixteen
hours in the back. Our mocha sauce is melted chocolate, not powder. If we can
make it ourselves and make it better, we do.

## Traverse City

We're a Northern Michigan shop through and through — built for the regulars who
come in before work, the summer crowd wandering Front Street, and everyone who
finds the mobile cart parked at a farmers market or a wedding. Come say hi.
