## Buy ten, the eleventh is on us

Our rewards program is about as complicated as a punch card, because it basically
is one — just without the paper card that lives in your glovebox and never gets
punched. Sign up once, check in at the counter, and every tenth drink is free.

No points math, no tiers, no expiring balances. Coffee, then more coffee, then a
free one.
